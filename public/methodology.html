Our approach

Property affordability

For modelled bank financing, the engine calculates monthly debt headroom using the 55% TDSR cap, and applies the 30% MSR cap to HDB and eligible new EC purchases. It estimates loan capacity from both monthly repayments and a tiered LTV ceiling, using the lower purchase price or valuation as collateral basis.

Selected financing rate and tenure determine the amortisation factor. Banks may apply higher stress-test rates, different income recognition and additional credit checks. The output is not a loan approval.

Stamp duty and upfront funding

Residential BSD uses IRAS progressive bands (1%, 2%, 3%, 4%, 5%, 6%) on the higher of price or market value. ABSD is estimated by buyer residency and property count. Joint purchasers, exemptions, remissions, CPF rules, HDB eligibility, grants, minimum cash down payments and special ownership arrangements are not modelled. Upfront funding is a broad cash/CPF requirement estimate, not an all-cash requirement.

Vehicle affordability

The model uses an OMV threshold of S$20,000: 70% maximum LTV at or below the threshold, 60% above it, with a 7-year maximum tenure for regulated individual financing. Loan repayment uses a reducing-balance rate, which is not interchangeable with a dealer's flat-rate quote. Enter a complete on-road price including COE and relevant taxes. Vehicle price, not OMV, is used as the LTV base.

Understanding ownership cost

Our planning-horizon metric is cumulative cash outflows: upfront funds, loan payments during the selected horizon and estimated running expenses. It is not an economic net TCO after resale, because it excludes property/vehicle residual value, appreciation or depreciation, sale proceeds and some transaction costs. Remaining loan balance is shown separately.

Privacy, reliability and scope

Calculations are submitted to the server but not stored in a database. Avoid entering personal identifiers. Input bounds are validated on the server. Rates and regulations can change, and the regulatory model covers only explicitly supported scenarios. Confirm current rules with the authorities and lender before making commitments.
