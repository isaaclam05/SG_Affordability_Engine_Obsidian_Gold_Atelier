About the platform

Financial clarity, built for Singapore.

SG Affordability Engine is an independent portfolio project exploring the intersection of financial regulation, financial modelling and thoughtful product engineering.

Why this exists

A headline purchase price tells only part of the story. Property and vehicle purchases involve financing limits, interest, taxes, down payments and ongoing commitments. This platform brings these assumptions together in one transparent planning tool.

Technology

Vanilla JavaScript and Chart.js frontend, Node.js/Express API, and a separate Python computation engine. The application is stateless and deployable with Docker.

Important limitation

This is not a bank, government service, financial adviser or property agent. No actual credit checks or approval decisions are performed. Calculations are illustrative and are not financial advice.
